About the group

A place to connect, learn and share

The UK Interest Group on Knowledge Graphs brings together people interested in the foundations, methods and applications of knowledge graphs. Relaunched with support from the Open Data Institute, the group builds on the UK community established through The Alan Turing Institute.

Our activities create space for discussion, collaboration and resource sharing across academia, industry, government and the wider community.
